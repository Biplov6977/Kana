HOW TO RE-SKIN THIS DEMO FOR THE NEXT CAFE
==========================================

You only touch TWO things.

1) config.js
   Change:
   - name, tagline, parent, neighbourhood, hook
   - story paragraphs
   - address, phone, instagram, maps, booking
   - hours (openHour / closeHour as 24h numbers)
   - theme colors (ink, deep, forest, sage, tile, cream, gold)
   - highlights and notes
   - photo paths if you rename files

2) images/
   Replace these 6 files with the new cafe's photos
   (keep the same filenames and you don't even edit config):

   hero-bar.jpg        → their counter / sign / best interior
   garden.jpg          → seating / room
   oven.jpg            → a second space shot
   entrance-night.jpg  → facade / door / night
   table-spread.jpg    → food on a table
   pizza.jpg           → hero food plate

COLOR SHORTCUTS
---------------
Garden / foliage cafe  → keep the current greens
Dark espresso bar      → ink #0e0c0a, deep #1a1410, tile #c4a574
Pink pastry house      → deep #2a1820, tile #e7b7b0, cream #f7eee8
Coastal white          → deep #1c2424, tile #9eb7b2, cream #f6f3ea

SENDING IT
----------
Zip the whole kana-demo folder and send it.
They can open index.html in a browser.
Or drop the folder on Netlify / Cloudflare Pages for a live URL.

Photos used here are public listing photos of Kana
(Zomato / District / Swiggy / Urbanaut). If the owner
gives you official shots, drop them in /images and it
will look even more like their house.
